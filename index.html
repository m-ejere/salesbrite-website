The problem

Every missed call is a job that walks to someone else.

None of this is unusual — it's the default state of a busy trade business. The cost just doesn't show up on an invoice.

01

Missed calls = lost jobs

You're on a roof, under a sink, or mid-install. The phone rings out. The caller doesn't leave a voicemail — they call the next number on the search results page instead.

Most calls to trade businesses go unanswered during working hours.
02

Slow quoting = lost customers

A customer asks three tradespeople for a quote. Whoever replies first, with a clear price and a slot, usually gets the job — regardless of who's actually best.

Response speed often matters more than price.
03

Admin overload = wasted time

Evenings and weekends go on calling people back, chasing details, and rebuilding the diary by hand — time that should be billable or off the clock entirely.

Hours spent on admin instead of paid work.
The system

One system, four jobs done automatically.

It sits behind your existing phone number and works the same whether it's 9am or midnight, weekday or bank holiday.

Answers calls automatically

Picks up every call instantly, greets the customer in a natural voice, and never sends a job to voicemail.

Captures job details

Asks the right questions for the trade — fault, location, access, urgency — and writes it all down accurately.

Books appointments

Checks your live availability and slots the job straight into the diary — no double bookings, no back-and-forth.

Sends confirmations

Texts the customer their appointment time and details immediately, so they're not left wondering if it actually went through.
